## Conditioning and independence

Initial states for **2,538 initial +81 associated constructors** are inherited from the immutable independently reconstructed [initial](f1-initial-crossing.md) and [associated](f1-crossing-constructor.md) receipts. Their generated times, directions, positions, status, line anchor and private offset are joined by full object identity.

**The 2,471 material-only constructor CA states, line anchors and private offsets remain explicit captured inputs.** Their numerical construction/discovery is not independently reconstructed here. All tube endpoints are supplied raw target-specific geometry; native trajectory evolution and accepted object/record membership are supplied conditioning. Consequently the material-origin exhausted search is an independent *later search from a captured initial condition*, not independent material discovery.

After initialization, all later hints and retained CA states are recursively generated. No later recorded hints, times, status, visit indices, reference selection or CA vectors feed a subsequent search. Recorded outputs enter comparison only. Native fitted trajectories are not generated by this replay.

## Ordering and source semantics

- Object keys are `(run, subrun, event, iseed, object)`; order is target-local `sequence`. Never order by effect time or infer lifecycle from iteration ordinals.
- Trajectory keys include full target and `trajectory_index`, with explicit piece index/range. Stage-2 wrapper calls use category-3 contexts; stage-1 calls use category-4 candidate or category-5 replacement contexts. This sample contains 289 category-3, 3,373 category-4 and **zero category-5** contexts.
- Stage-3 prefilter and stage-4 partner-mutation records retain the preceding searched state. Their current `trajectory_index` need not identify that retained state's reference context.
- The unchanged `f1_later_ca.piecewise` uses `float(np.float32(1e-4))`, ten moves, earlier-piece boundary ties and source pointer-before-stop semantics. The configuration's absent `XingReferencePiece` and pinned `MetaIterConfig` defaults establish DOCA/ten moves; captured precision/caps are comparison guards, not parameter tuning.
- The hint is generated from the preceding usable state through `crossing_hint`. A generated unusable state fails the entire replay rather than silently using captured fallback times or excluding a lane.
- Per-piece trace replay follows **generated visit indices only**, calling the unchanged local solver in batched solve depths. Generated outgoing times feed the next solve; captured visit indices and times never drive replay.
- The final reference pointer can differ from the last solved piece. Cap demotion affects the final status only; the per-piece trace retains the pre-demotion local status.
- Constructor private offsets stay fixed as reference momentum changes. Effect time is generated particle TOCA plus that fixed offset, never a newly computed speed quotient or inferred time difference.
- Partner persistence is compared by propagating supplied constructor/setHit identities. No final-collection partner stamps are used. In this sample no mutation occurs; autonomous hit association, fallback hit-CA lifecycle and activity are outside the claim.

## Exact compared quantities

Across all records: particle/sensor CA times and positions, directions, status/usability, selected reference parameters/range, effect time, fixed offset, partner persistence, line start/end/direction/length/anchor.

Across all later searches: own-hint kind, incoming particle/sensor hints, initial/final/last-solved indices, move count/exhaustion, full visited-piece sequence and per-piece incoming hints, solved times, status and usability.

**Not compared:** cached CA derivatives/variances, every internal local-iteration arithmetic update, material lengths/effects or active-state policy. Selected trajectory parameters/range are checked against supplied trajectories, not independently fitted ones. Full fit/F1/numerical/discovery and complete-history flags remain false.
